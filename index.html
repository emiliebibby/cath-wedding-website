Good to Know

Questions, answered

Can I bring a plus-one?+

Your invitation names everyone we've saved a seat for. If you spot a "+ guest", the extra chair is yours to fill.

Are children welcome?+

We adore your little ones, but we've planned an adults-only celebration so everyone can let their hair down. Named children on your invite are of course included.

What's the dress code?+

Garden formal — think summer suits and floaty dresses. The ceremony and drinks are on grass, so block heels or flats will thank you.

Is it indoors or out?+

Both — the ceremony is in the walled garden, the reception in a glasshouse. There's cover if the British weather has other ideas.

Where do I park or stay?+

Free on-site parking, plus two hotels holding rooms for us — see the Travel & Stay section above.

Everything to include & how we get there

A little gift for your friend — the full checklist of what a lovely one-page wedding site needs, a build roadmap, and how to put it online. Delete this whole section before going live.

1 · What to include

  • Names, date & place The first thing a guest sees
  • Countdown or save-the-date A little anticipation
  • Your story How you met, the proposal
  • Ceremony & reception details Times, addresses, dress code
  • Schedule of the day So no one misses the confetti
  • Travel & parking Directions, shuttle, taxis
  • Accommodation Hotel blocks & rates
  • RSVP form The one thing that must work
  • Dietary & song requests Fold into the RSVP
  • Registry / honeymoon fund Framed gently
  • FAQ Plus-ones, kids, weather, dress
  • Photo gallery A few favourites
  • Contact for questions An email or point person
  • Wedding hashtag For sharing photos after

2 · How we get there

1
Gather · ~1 hour together

Collect the real details

Names, date, venue addresses, times, dress code, hotels, RSVP deadline, registry links, and 4–8 favourite photos. This mockup is the fill-in-the-blanks form — anything in a placeholder needs a real answer.

2
Personalise · content

Swap placeholders for their story

Replace the text, dates and photos. Their sage-green palette already lives here — every colour is a variable at the very top (--sage, --blush…), so re-tinting is a one-line change.

3
Wire up · the RSVP

Make replies land somewhere

Point the form at Google Forms, Formspree or Tally so responses drop into a spreadsheet. This is the only truly functional piece — everything else is presentation.

4
Publish · go live

Put it on the internet

Drag the folder onto Netlify or push to GitHub Pages (both free). Optionally point a pretty domain like catherineandcharlie.co.uk at it.

5
Polish · before sharing

Test & send

Check it on a phone, click every link, send yourself a test RSVP, then share the link on the invitations. Delete this planning section first!

3 · Ways to put it online

Easiest · free

Netlify Drop

Drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop — live in seconds with a shareable link. No account needed to start.

Best for getting it live today.

Free · you're in git

GitHub Pages

This is already a repo. Push it, enable Pages in settings, and it's hosted at a github.io URL — updates on every commit.

Best for tinkering over time.

~£10/yr · polished

Custom domain

Buy a name (Namecheap, Google Domains) and point it at Netlify or Pages so guests get a memorable address.

Best for the finishing touch.

One self-contained HTML file — no build tools, no dependencies. Fonts & florals load in any browser online.
