Good to Know

Questions, answered

Can I bring a plus-one?+

Everyone who's invited is named on the envelope of your invitation, so we're not able to offer plus-ones.

Are children welcome?+

We adore your little ones, but we've planned an adults-only celebration so everyone can let their hair down. Named children on your invite are of course included.

When do I need to reply by?+

Please reply by the thirty-first of January, 2027. Scan the QR code on your invitation, or use the RSVP page.

I'm an evening guest. When should I arrive?+

Evening guests are invited from quarter to seven, in time for the first dance at half past seven.

What's the dress code?+

Cocktail / smart. The ceremony will be held on a grassy lawn (weather dependent), so please choose your footwear accordingly.

Is it indoors or out?+

The ceremony will be held on a grassy lawn (weather dependent), and the mini golf is outside too.

Where do I park or stay?+

Parking is free on-site. The Holt Fleet and The Wharf Inn are nearby; see the Travel & Stay section above.
